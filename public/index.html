Clear responsibilities.
Privacy & evidence
The evidence tools process your chosen file locally. Wallet connection details stay in page memory; wallet signature verification sends the selected public address and signature to this server. Public capability requests are normal web requests and may appear in hosting logs. Do not enter passwords, private keys, or confidential evidence into diagnostic requests.
Rights & authority
A wallet signature, hash, token, or technical record does not establish legal title or grant a license. Policy evaluation uses configured rules; it does not automatically adopt laws or issue a legal determination. Legal, rights, and commercial actions follow the relevant agreements and authorized approval paths.
Available architecture
Salted commitments are not zero-knowledge proofs. Chain adapters do not create a deployed native chain. Token issuance, public-chain settlement, production ZK proofs, and paid fulfillment require separately verified deployments. Live status reports exactly what this installation exposes.
Source & installation terms
Source visibility follows CrownThrive’s proprietary repository license. Commercial hosting, redistribution, and white-label rights follow the relevant written agreement.
