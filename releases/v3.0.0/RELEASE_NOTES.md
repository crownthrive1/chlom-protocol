# CHLOM V3.0.0 — Phase 3 Governed Protocol

**Release date:** 2026-08-26  
**Institutional generation:** Phase 3

## Summary

CHLOM V3.0.0 establishes the repository's Phase 3 institutional generation and replaces the stale root-level Phase 2/pre-alpha framing. It preserves all prior papers, contracts, schemas, services, and evidence according to their own version and lifecycle state.

## Compatibility

`v3.0.0` is the **repository generation**, not an instruction to renumber every internal component. Existing v1/v2 schemas, contracts, services, APIs, and evidence records remain valid until their own version lifecycle supersedes them.

## Source-of-truth rule

CrownThrive OS governs CrownThrive-wide institutional state. CHLOM records govern their exact component scope. Mintlify, websites, and other publication surfaces are downstream projections and do not redefine current OS/CHLOM state.

## Authority boundaries

- `HOLD` is not converted to `PASS` by release/version promotion.
- Provider capability is not CrownThrive provider-write authority.
- Operation-level certification does not grant provider-wide mutation authority.
- D3/new sovereign authority remains human-reserved unless separately changed by governing process.
- Historical records remain preserved and do not silently become current.

## Result

CHLOM now has a Phase 3 front door, governance shell, archive model, rights protection layer, and release lineage aligned with CrownThrive OS while preserving component-level semantic-version truth.
